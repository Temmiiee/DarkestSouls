# Playtest checklist

## Main question

After seeing a card's effect and cost a few times, can players predict its hidden stamina cost before resolving it?

## Check during a run

- Can a new player understand that costs are hidden until first use?
- Do players notice that the queue resolves FIFO?
- Does placing Reprendre souffle before Frappe lourde feel meaningfully different from placing it after?
- Do players correctly anticipate when a queued card will fail for lack of stamina?
- Is the breath bar legible enough for players to feel depletion and recovery without a numeric total?
- Do Guard and Dodge feel distinct when the enemy telegraphs its attack?
- After a win or defeat, can the player explain which choices led there?
- Does stamina carry over into the second bout without a refill?
- Does the repeated opponent still feel like one continuous duel rather than a reset?
- Does the HUD avoid numeric health, stamina, damage, turn, and queue counters while still giving enough feedback to act?
- Can players infer danger from the Veilleur's posture without seeing exact incoming damage?
- Do ember marks teach relative card costs without making the fight feel like a calculation screen?
- Are feedback messages brief, contextual, and free of damage totals?

Record each player's predicted cost before revealing the card, then compare it with the fixed cost. The browser slice has no analytics; collect answers manually.
